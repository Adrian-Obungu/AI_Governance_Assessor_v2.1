# Limitations

- The assessor organizes evidence and scoring; it does not certify compliance or provide legal advice.
- Framework mappings require human review and may change as NIST, ISO, EU, and other requirements evolve.
- Scores are only as reliable as the evidence and answers supplied by the assessor.
- The current repository is a self-hosted development project, not a managed multi-tenant service.
- Deployment hardening, TLS termination, backups, monitoring, and identity-provider integration remain operator responsibilities.
- The project has not been independently penetration-tested.
- PDF and CSV exports may contain confidential assessment information and require controlled handling.
- Sample configurations are for local development only; all default secrets and credentials must be replaced before deployment.
- Compatibility and performance at large organizational scale have not yet been established.
